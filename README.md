# x402 on Base: due diligence, October 2026

Research on the projects building for x402, the open payment protocol built on HTTP 402, on the Base network. For each project it covers what it does, who runs it, how it is funded, whether it has kept shipping, whether it chases trends, and who it competes with. It also covers what Coinbase and Cloudflare have actually shipped, how to read x402 usage numbers, and a plan for tracking the space every day.

Research date: 11 October 2026. Treat everything here as a snapshot from that date.

## Start here

- [report.md](report.md) is the main report: the bottom line, a league table of every project, 17 detailed profiles, competitors and threats, and the daily tracking plan.
- [profiles/](profiles/) holds condensed due diligence on every project, grouped by type, with sources and tracking links.
- [notes/](notes/) holds the underlying research notes, with full source lists.

## How it was made

AI research agents (Claude) worked from public sources: project sites and docs, GitHub through its API, x402scan, Coinbase's x402 Bazaar, CoinGecko, news coverage and company filings. Claims link to their sources. Verdicts, scores and opinion lines are judgements, not facts.

## Limits

- The agents could not open x.com, so no X account was checked for recent posts.
- x402scan counts settled USDC transfers, which mixes real purchases with automated and incentivised traffic. Section 3 of the report explains how to read the numbers.
- A few profiles are thin (t54 labs, Pocket Network, x402.boats) because the research stopped early.
- Figures and product statuses in this space change quickly. Check the sources before relying on them.

## People

Profiles name founders, leads and engineers only from public, professional sources: official team pages, announcements, a person's own GitHub profile, or verified links. No attempt was made to identify pseudonymous people. If you are named here and want a correction or removal, open an issue.
