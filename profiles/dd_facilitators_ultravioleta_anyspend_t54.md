## Ultravioleta DAO

### One-line
A Wyoming-registered, Latin American DAO that began as a Twitch streaming community (March 2022) and now runs a free, multi-chain x402 facilitator written in Rust (a heavily extended fork of the open-source x402-rs), plus SDKs, an ERC-8004 agent-reputation relay, escrow/refund contracts, a curated catalogue of paid endpoints, and a handful of agent apps. One pseudonymous engineer ("0xultravioleta") writes almost all the code, with AI coding tools.
Sources: [DAO about page](https://ultravioletadao.xyz/about) (fetched via r.jina.ai because the host was blocked on my network), [facilitator homepage](https://facilitator.ultravioletadao.xyz) (same method), [x402-rs fork](https://github.com/UltravioletaDAO/x402-rs).

### x402 role
- Facilitator (primary): verify/settle/accepts endpoints, 0% fee, no API key ([homepage](https://facilitator.ultravioletadao.xyz), via r.jina.ai).
- SDK/tooling: `uvd-x402-sdk` on [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/uvd-x402-sdk/) (118 releases, latest 0.97.0 on 2026-10-11) and [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/uvd-x402-sdk) (111 versions, latest 2.104.1 on 2026-10-11) ([PyPI JSON](https://pypi.org/pypi/uvd-x402-sdk/json), [npm registry](https://registry.npmjs.org/uvd-x402-sdk)).
- Discovery: a "Curated Bazaar" that aggregates paid endpoints from other facilitators and probes them; 1,452 listed and "verified alive" on 2026-10-11 ([/discovery/stats](https://facilitator.ultravioletadao.xyz/discovery/stats), via r.jina.ai). Self-reported.
- Trust/identity: an ERC-8004 identity/reputation relay (registers agents, submits feedback, pays the gas) across 23 networks, and x402r-style escrow/refund contracts on 10 networks ([homepage](https://facilitator.ultravioletadao.xyz), via r.jina.ai).
- Also a seller/app builder: Execution Market (task marketplace), KarmaKadabra (agent swarm), MeshRelay, Describe.net, Emporium and others, all paying through its own facilitator ([ecosystem page](https://ultravioletadao.xyz/ecosystem), via r.jina.ai).
- Not a token experiment in the x402 sense: the DAO has a governance token (UVD) but it is not wired into the facilitator (see Token).

### Pace verdict
Not slow; the opposite. For a one-person team it is extremely fast: about 157 version bumps in five months, 553 facilitator commits, 117 SDK releases on PyPI and npm combined since April, and 9 products on the ecosystem map. Compared with the category reference, the x402 reference repo ([x402-foundation/x402](https://github.com/x402-foundation/x402)) logged about 90 commits a month from 122 authors between April and October (bare clone, my count), and the upstream Rust library the fork came from ([x402-rs/x402-rs](https://github.com/x402-rs/x402-rs)) made 125 commits between April and July and none since 2026-07-13. Against a small team using AI tools in 2026 (monthly or faster releases, weekly commits), Ultravioleta is well above that bar since July. Two caveats: April to June was quiet (42 facilitator commits in three months, one version bump in June), and the high commit rate is mostly AI-assisted single-developer output rather than a larger team, so bus factor is 1. October is cooling (36 commits and 3 version bumps in the first 11 days against 268 and 69 in September).

### Direction
Stated: "AI agent economies, x402 gasless payments, 8004" and "web4" ([GitHub profile README](https://github.com/UltravioletaDAO/UltravioletaDAO)); the site pitches "Payment infrastructure for autonomous agents" and nine ecosystem products. Shipped: matches the pitch (facilitator breadth, ERC-8004 relay, escrow, MCP, evidence, Bazaar). Gaps: no outside customers visible; the product portfolio is wider than one engineer can maintain; the Zama FHE scheme was added and switched off within weeks (decision log in v2.49.0), which shows the team follows new standards quickly. Expect continued chain additions (Arc, Hedera, XRPL, Robinhood) and agent-registry features rather than a focus on one revenue line; there is no fee, so no business model beyond the DAO treasury and token.

Coinbase CDP is far ahead (118M tx all time; 1.97M tx and $233K on Base in the last 30 days per the ecosystem map's x402scan pull). Other multi-chain or free facilitators: PayAI (24.3K tx / $5.9K on Base in 30 days), thirdweb, OpenX402, x402.rs (Rust, upstream), FluxA (137K tx / $322K in 30 days), OpenFacilitator, Dexter, Polymer, Primer ([ecosystem notes](ecosystem_map_and_metrics.md), numbers re-checked for Ultravioleta only). Ultravioleta's differentiators are chain breadth (22+ mainnets), ERC-8004 relay, escrow and the curated Bazaar; its weakness is that it shows no organic third-party volume.

### Green and red flags
Green:
- Unusually transparent stats page that separates recorded from reconstructed numbers and states its own limits ([/stats](https://facilitator.ultravioletadao.xyz/stats)).
- Public repos, a real changelog, 100+ package releases each on PyPI and npm, a live and healthy service on 2026-10-11.
- Listed as a facilitator by Avalanche's and SKALE's developer docs; invited as hackathon mentor.
- Legal wrapper (Wyoming DAO LLC, self-reported) and a four-year community history.
Red:
- Recent Base volume is overwhelmingly scripted (BotPay mint/video loops, fan-out wallets), and the "fast growth" is a burst that ended on 2026-10-08.
- Bus factor of one; 89% of commits AI co-authored; very large unreviewed-looking commit volume.
- 2024 token incident ("contract drained") and a token with no function in the x402 product; token incentives (airdrops, wheel) run alongside.
- No outside funding found, no named founder, no revenue (0% fee).
- Scope sprawl (nine products, 22+ chains) and "claims" that move (19, 22, 23, 29, 33+ networks across SKALE post, README, SDK and homepage).
- GitHub presence is a personal-type account, not an organisation; no GitHub Releases.

### Verdict
Credible but small. Momentum: Steady (accelerated July to September, cooling in October; usage fell back to near zero after 2026-10-08). This is a real piece of engineering by a committed, pseudonymous developer working with AI tools, run through a community DAO with a long history, and the shipping record backs the stated direction. It is not a source of x402 demand: on the evidence of 37K Base transfers, almost nothing flowing through it is organic, and the headline "25.5K transactions in 30 days" says more about a few scripted wallets and a BotPay promotion than about adoption. I would follow it for what it ships (new chains, ERC-8004, MCP, escrow, DX402) rather than for volume, and treat any future spike in its x402scan numbers as suspect until the wallets are checked. Where it is heading: more chain integrations and agent-identity features, a small ecosystem of its own agent apps, and continued dependence on one developer.

### Pace verdict
Slow for x402, and plainly so: no commit, no package release and no product note on x402 for eight months, against daily releases from Ultravioleta and about 90 commits a month on the x402 reference repo. The company itself is not slow (a new business line launched in August, 30 staff claimed), but the work is not happening on this product, and the public repos hold only a fraction of the real code, so GitHub understates B3's overall activity. A small AI-assisted team in 2026 would ship monthly; the x402 packages have not been touched since 2026-02-04.

### Direction
Stated: AnySpend as "payment infrastructure for crypto" and a future "AnySpend V2: DeFi automation engine" ([docs home](https://docs.anyspend.com/), [Tokenomics](https://docs.anyspend.com/anyspend/tokenomics)); the group site frames B3 as B3OS plus Labs plus Holdings, and the newest messaging is AI compute and agents ([b3labs.org](https://b3labs.org)). Shipped: B3IQ. None of the August 2026 launch coverage (Fortune, Cryptonews) mentions AnySpend, x402 or the ANY token. Expect AnySpend to continue as a swap/checkout utility for B3's other products and the x402 facilitator to stay dormant unless the marketplace bots return.

### Competitors
Anyone running a Base x402 facilitator is ahead on organic use: Coinbase CDP (1.97M tx / $233K on Base in the last 30 days), FluxA (137K / $322K), PayAI (24.3K / $5.9K), plus OpenX402, Meridian, Polymer, Primer, thirdweb and Ultravioleta DAO (see its profile above; its volume is also mostly scripted) ([ecosystem notes](ecosystem_map_and_metrics.md)). ### Green and red flags
Green:
- Real, funded company with named Coinbase-alumni founders, about $21M raised, around 30 staff and an actual new product (B3IQ) with press coverage.
- Clear docs for the facilitator, tokenomics and airdrop rules; open-source middleware with 94 commits of real work in Oct 2025 to Feb 2026.
- The facilitator endpoint still responds and still lists nine networks.
Red:
- 99.9% of recorded x402 volume is one of its own marketplace addresses, paid by about 260 wallets that never used any other facilitator or seller; the stop on 2026-08-17 looks like a switch-off.
- No x402 code or package change since 2026-02-04; the old `docs.b3.fun/anyspend/x402-overview` page now returns 404 and the docs moved to docs.anyspend.com.
- ANY: usage-for-points airdrop design; 93.8% of supply in ten unlabelled wallets; claimed buyback-and-burn not visible in supply; no independent evidence that fee discounts or staking are live; "19+ networks" claim against nine advertised.
- Parent company pivoted from gaming to payments to workflow automation to AI hardware within about two and a half years.
- Closed-source core; GitHub activity is not a reliable measure.

### Verdict
Likely abandoned (as an x402 facilitator); momentum Stalled. The company behind it, B3, is a funded, credible builder, but its attention and its public launches have moved to B3IQ's GPU business, and the x402 line shows eight months without a code or package change and a flat-lined, machine-like payment pattern that stopped on 2026-08-17. The headline "521K transactions and $141K" should not be read as adoption: it is B3 paying B3's own ad marketplace through its own facilitator. ANY is an airdrop-and-points usage token with concentrated holdings, not something the x402 product needs. I would not track it daily; a check once a month for a restart (new payers, a release on npm, or a change in the x402scan facilitator row) is enough.
