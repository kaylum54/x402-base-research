# Digest: competition, rival rails and chains (x402 on Base due diligence)

Source: `competition_rival_rails_chains.md` (researcher notes as of 2026-10-11; window 2026-04-11 to 2026-10-11; rivalry only). Labels from the notes: "vendor claim" = a company describing its own product; "search-index excerpt" = text seen only in search results because the page would not load. The researcher's search budget ran out part-way, so later checks were direct page fetches. Every figure is the researcher's; none was rechecked here.

## 1. Bottom line

On transaction counts, x402 on Base is losing ground: Solana passed Base on daily count on 2026-08-25 ([Solana Compass 08-25](https://solanacompass.com/news/solana-flips-base-in-daily-x402-transactions-for-the-first-time-in-six-months)) and carried 76% of x402 transactions in the four weeks to 2026-09-22, on Artemis data ([Solana Compass 09-22](https://solanacompass.com/news/solana-processes-76-of-all-x402-ai-agent-transactions-232-million-in-four-weeks)). On wash-filtered dollars the loss is unproven: the last public split (Visa/Artemis, data to 2026-04-21) had Base at about 93% of adjusted volume ([The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/infrastructure/visa-s-sheffield-pegs-adjusted-x402-volume-at-19m)), and Coinbase's Q2 results, as reported on 2026-07-30, put more than 90% of agentic stablecoin volume on Base, though that report cites no filing ([Forkast](https://forkast.news/coinbases-q2-miss-hides-the-first-public-proof-of-where-agent-payments-settle/)). Nobody has published a wash-filtered split since April, so Base is losing the count race and has not been shown to be losing the dollar race. The count shift looks like a few routing decisions (BlockRun's gateway, Ramp's agent-wallet alpha on Solana, AWS CloudFront's pay-per-AI-crawler integration) rather than broad adoption, and a lead of that kind is reversible: Solana's share swung between 49.7% and 88% earlier in 2026 ([Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/solana-x402-market-share-dominance/)). The rival that matters most at protocol level is Stripe and Tempo's Machine Payments Protocol (MPP), which is backwards-compatible with x402. Stripe's docs steer merchants to MPP first and support x402 on Base only, while MPP runs on Tempo and Solana ([Stripe docs](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/machine)). MPP's measured agent usage is small (about $25,000 over about 115,000 transactions as of 2026-04-21, per [Visa](https://www.visa.com/en-us/thought-leadership/innovation/agentic-payments-from-the-ground-up)), and Visa, Mastercard and Google, which hedge by sitting in several camps, have published little usage for their own agent-payment products. Facilitators are commoditising (Coinbase's CDP charges $0.001 per transaction after 1,000 free a month, against free alternatives) ([CDP facilitator docs](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/x402/core-concepts/facilitator)), so for projects that run only on Base the researcher sees narrative risk first (count leadership has moved) and revenue risk later (Stripe steering, buyer concentration).

## 2. Chain share over the last six months

### Reading the numbers
- x402.org (fetched 2026-10-11) shows 30-day totals of 75.41M transactions, $24.24M volume, 94.06K buyers and 22K sellers, stamped 2026-08-25, so about seven weeks stale ([x402.org](https://www.x402.org)). Cumulative estimates run from 120M to 187M transactions by tracker; the article reads the spread as measurement problems and possibly non-organic volume ([Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/solana-x402-market-share-dominance/)).
- Artemis flags some transactions as gamed: an average of 63.94% from 2025-11-09 to 2026-02-07, peaking at 90.21% on one day ([4pillars](https://research.4pillars.io/en/research/x402-on-polygon-real-or-gamed); search-index excerpt, the page returned HTTP 429).
- Visa/Artemis adjusted totals (wash and test activity excluded): about $15.0M volume and 109.6M transactions since the May 2025 launch, as of 2026-04-21 ([Visa](https://www.visa.com/en-us/thought-leadership/innovation/agentic-payments-from-the-ground-up)). A Visa executive's thread gave different totals (about $19M and 134M) plus the per-chain split below and about 4,000 wallets (the top 1% of buyers) behind about 90% of adjusted volume; only its headline was verified ([The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/infrastructure/visa-s-sheffield-pegs-adjusted-x402-volume-at-19m)).

- Ramp announced an x402 agent-wallet alpha on Solana on 2026-08-20 or 08-21 (articles differ) ([Solana Compass on Ramp](https://solanacompass.com/news/ramp-adds-x402-ai-agent-wallets-on-solana-giving-70000-businesses-autonomous-payment-capabilities)). Other Solana-settled programs named on 2026-09-22: AWS CloudFront's pay-per-AI-crawler integration, Messari and Nansen paid data, and Coinbase's own Solana facilitator, upgraded 2026-09-18 with `upto` support and a 66% cut in verification latency ([Solana Compass 09-22](https://solanacompass.com/news/solana-processes-76-of-all-x402-ai-agent-transactions-232-million-in-four-weeks)).
- No source gives a cause for Polygon's rise from 2026-01-18.

### Researcher's judgements
- Ticket size (researcher's arithmetic): Solana cumulative about $0.29 per transaction ($10M over 35M); x402.org 30-day about $0.32; Visa/Artemis adjusted about $0.14 ($15.0M over 109.6M). A chain that wins on count can still be small in dollars.
- 23.2M being 76% implies about 30.5M transactions in four weeks, far below x402.org's 75.41M for 30 days. They cannot both be total x402 activity; do not mix them in one chart.
- The real chain rivals have a distribution wedge: Solana (gateways, enterprise on-ramps), Tempo (Stripe's default chain), BNB Chain (Binance wallet distribution, agent-identity lead) and Arc (USDC-native chain plus Circle's batching). Avalanche, Sei, Kite and Pieverse show no volume evidence.

### MPP in detail (the protocol-level rival)
- Launched 2026-03-18 with Tempo mainnet, co-authored by Stripe and Tempo; a payment authentication scheme submitted to the IETF, with charge, session and subscription intents. Cloudflare's docs (modified 2026-08-05) call it backwards-compatible with x402 ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/394131/tempo-mainnet-goes-live-with-machine-payments-protocol-for-agents); [Cloudflare docs](https://developers.cloudflare.com/agents/agentic-payments/mpp)). x402 `exact` maps onto MPP `charge`, and one endpoint can serve both through mppx ([mpp.dev: MPP vs x402](https://mpp.dev/mpp-vs-x402)).
- Stripe's docs (2026-10-11): MPP runs on Tempo (USDC.e) and Solana (USDC); x402 on Base (USDC). Card payments have a $0.50 minimum and stablecoin payments $0.01 USDC; stablecoin payments are open to US businesses except in New York ([Stripe docs](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/machine)).
- Researcher: the threat is strategic, not yet volumetric. MPP reaches Stripe merchants (card plus stablecoin, with Stripe's tax, fraud and refund tools) and can serve x402 clients from the same endpoint; Tempo's large numbers are payout flows. A Stripe merchant who wants stablecoins and does not care about the protocol is steered toward MPP on non-Base rails.

### Researcher's judgements on the table
- The two High entries differ in kind: Solana is a measured share shift on counts; MPP/Tempo is a distribution and protocol challenge with little measured usage. BlockRun and Ramp are Medium because each is one routing decision large enough to move chain share, not because of company size.
- Skyfire, TollBit, L402 and Catena are unlikely to take volume from Base x402 projects in the next six months; Nevermined and OKX are more likely to ride on x402 than replace it.
## 4. Dynamics

### Card networks and stablecoins
- Visa's stablecoin settlement run rate was about $7B as of March 2026, with 160+ stablecoin-linked card programs ([Visa release 2026-06-10](https://investor.visa.com/news/news-details/2026/Visa-Announces-New-AI-Stablecoin-and-Token-Innovations-to-Power-Intelligent-Programmable-Commerce-at-Visa-Payments-Forum/default.aspx)). Visa and Artemis conclude the outcome will not come down to cards versus stablecoins: cards suit consumer purchases, stablecoins suit machine micropayments, and some tasks will use both ([Visa](https://www.visa.com/en-us/thought-leadership/innovation/agentic-payments-from-the-ground-up)).
- Mastercard agreed on 2026-03-17 to buy BVNK for up to $1.8B ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/03/17/mastercard-bvnk-acquisition-stablecoins-1-8-billion)) and built AP4M across cards, accounts and stablecoins; its 30+ launch partners include Coinbase, OKX, Polygon, Solana, Tempo and Cloudflare ([The Block](https://theblock.co/post/404288/mastercard-agent-pay-machines-support-autonomous-ai-transactions-stablecoins)). Solana Compass calls MPP a competing standard and says Mastercard works with Coinbase and OKX while they pursue independent standards ([Solana Compass](https://solanacompass.com/news/mastercard-launches-agent-pay-for-machines-with-solana-foundation-among-30-partners)).
- Researcher: card networks are a medium-term competitor for consumer-sized purchases and an ally for machine micropayments. Watch whether AP4M's credential and settlement layer becomes the default for agents spending for businesses; it could route around any single x402 chain. Agentic checkout may default to cards above micropayment size (Stripe's card floor is $0.50). Consumer agent checkout is fragmenting faster than machine payments: OpenAI moved Instant Checkout into merchant apps (reported 2026-03-06) and Stripe's 2026-04-29 recap names UCP, not ACP, for Google ([Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/03/06/openai-shifts-checkout-plans-agentic-commerce-strategy/); [Stripe](https://stripe.com/blog/everything-we-announced-at-sessions-2026)).

### Standardising or fragmenting
- The Linux Foundation announced the x402 Foundation on 2026-04-02 with 22 organisations expressing intent, Base among them, and no rival protocol named ([Linux Foundation](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-is-launching-the-x402-foundation-and-welcoming-the-contribution-of-the-x402-protocol)). The 2026-07-14 operational launch had 40 founding organisations and 17 premier members, including Visa, Mastercard, Stripe, Google, AWS and Circle; Base is not named in that article, and PayPal and OpenAI are not in the lists retrieved ([Solana Compass 07-15](https://solanacompass.com/news/linux-foundation-launches-x402-foundation-as-open-standards-body-for-ai-agent-payments)).
- The same companies back rival stacks: Stripe co-authors MPP and ACP; Visa contributes to MPP and runs TAP; Mastercard is an MPP design partner and runs AP4M; Google runs AP2 and UCP. Stripe's docs list both MPP and x402 ([Stripe docs](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/machine)). mppx emits both x402 and MPP challenges from one endpoint, and Faremeter, Kite, Nevermined and OKX APP support both.
- Researcher: the likely end state is a 402 meta-layer where servers advertise several payment methods and clients pick one. Durable value sits in discovery (marketplaces), identity and spend controls, and billing, not the chain. Base-only projects that sell a chain-specific facilitator or a Base-only wallet are the most exposed.

### Facilitator commoditisation
- Coinbase's CDP facilitator: first 1,000 onchain transactions a month free, then $0.001 each; networks Base, Polygon, Arbitrum, World and Solana ([CDP network support](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/x402/network-support); [CDP facilitator docs](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/x402/core-concepts/facilitator)). Free alternatives in the x402 docs include Dexter, Built on Stellar, Mogami (Base), Polygon's own, NEAR, PayAI, Corbits, Celo and thirdweb ([x402 docs](https://docs.x402.org/dev-tools/facilitators)). Binance's B402 sponsors gas and matches the CDP v2 spec, so CDP-built clients need only a URL change ([Binance docs](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/products/onchainpay-x402/introduction)).
- Coinbase held 60 to 70% of daily transactions in October to early November 2025, then a quarter to a third once Dexter passed it (reported January 2026; search-index excerpt) ([Tangem](https://tangem.com/en/news/adoption/8246-dexter-overtakes-coinbase-as-top-x402-transaction-facilitator/)). No later facilitator-share data.
- Researcher: pricing power is low. Sellers pricing at or below $0.001 per call will not cover a CDP fee outside batch settlement (researcher's arithmetic).

### Risks specific to Base-only projects
- Count share: Base about 10% of transactions in the week to 2026-08-31; Solana 76% over four weeks to 2026-09-22 ([Coin-Turk](https://en.coin-turk.com/solana-overtakes-base-with-90yuzde-share-in-x402-transaction-volume-and-count/); [Solana Compass 09-22](https://solanacompass.com/news/solana-processes-76-of-all-x402-ai-agent-transactions-232-million-in-four-weeks)).
- Buyer concentration and wash activity: about 4,000 wallets drive about 90% of adjusted volume; gamed transactions averaged 63.94% from 2025-11-09 to 2026-02-07 ([The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/infrastructure/visa-s-sheffield-pegs-adjusted-x402-volume-at-19m); [4pillars](https://research.4pillars.io/en/research/x402-on-polygon-real-or-gamed)).
- Stripe steering: x402 on Base only, MPP on Tempo and Solana, Tempo as Stripe's default chain ([Stripe docs](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/machine)).
- Portability and neutrality: facilitators and SDKs already span many chains (Dexter eleven, CDP five, nine chain families in the reference repo) ([Dexter](https://dexter.cash)); x402 is no longer Coinbase's repo, and Base is not named as a Foundation member in the July article.
- Session billing: MPP sessions are built in; x402's `batch-settlement` is EVM-only and relatively new ([QuickNode](https://www.quicknode.com/blog/x402-vs-mpp); [x402 repo](https://github.com/x402-foundation/x402)).
- Researcher: narrative risk first (count leadership has moved), revenue risk later (Stripe steering, buyer concentration). The dollar-share evidence still favours Base as of April and per Coinbase's Q2 claim.
