# Digest: the x402 token wave on Base (source: dd_token_experiments.md, research date 2026-10-11)

This digest stands in for the raw notes. Dates, numbers, contract addresses and X handles are copied as written, with the notes' verification wording. The researcher's judgements are kept; "unconfirmed" and "not found" stay as they are. "Last six months" is 2026-04-11 to 2026-10-11. Token facts only, no price or chart analysis.
Scales in the notes. Verdict: Serious builder, Credible but small, Speculative, Likely abandoned. Momentum: Accelerating, Steady, Slowing, Stalled, Dead. Trend-hopping score: 1 = long-running focus where x402 is a natural extension; 3 = several pivots but a real product each time; 5 = narrative-only; no project scored 1.

## 1. Bottom line (tightened)

1. PING is an experiment with no builder to profile: an anonymous deployer, no team, no product, and nothing built since the mint ended on 2025-10-27 (the mint API host is gone from DNS).
2. An operator-run minter account, not the contract, credited tokens in batches of about 50 to 80 payments; the contract credited exactly 100,000 mints against 138,465 payments counted by x402scan.
3. $100,000 of PING's USDC went into the Uniswap pool, and $77,574 was withdrawn on 2025-11-17 by an account the deployer had made an admin minutes earlier; two accounts still hold the admin role.
4. The template was copied within a week (2025-10-24 to 2025-10-27): four contracts reuse its source, one is a factory variant and one a plain ERC-20 collection box, and all seven are dormant. About $603k of USDC left them, 25% into Uniswap pools and about 75% ($453k) withdrawn by admin or owner accounts between 2025-10-25 and 2025-11-17.
5. No launch tool has become durable: x420.dev has been stalled since 2025-11-12, Heurist's X402 Vending Machine made 20 launches in three weeks and none since, Covalent's SpeedRun shows a maintenance notice, x402.team is a meme and APINow.fun has one tokenized API plus its own token; only Regents Labs has heavy 2026 engineering, and x402 is peripheral to it.
6. Verified mint payees were about 25% of Base x402 transactions in the week of 2025-10-20 (about 50% counting probable mints), 97% on PING's peak day (2025-10-22), about 1% the week after and about 0% over the last 30 days; the much larger November 2025 surge (64.3M Base transactions) was not mints.
7. CoinGecko's x402 Ecosystem category held 135 coins, 62 with a Base contract; of 47 triaged (plus 6 large multi-chain tokens), 2 are Serious builder, 20 Credible but small, 19 Speculative and 5 Likely abandoned, plus TRANSLATE, the token of a profiled product.
8. Most are older AI-agent tokens with an x402 tag (15 predate x402's launch on 2025-05-06 and 15 more predate PING), and apart from AInalyst's click-game endpoint and AnySpend's facilitator, no triaged token's own x402 revenue that the researcher could measure exceeded $1,000.
9. A separate sweep tabulated 127 x402- or 402-named pools on Base, 96 of them Clanker deployments, and the researcher found no x402 mint integration at Clanker or Zora themselves.
10. On the 1-5 narrative-hopping rubric, 63 profiles score 11 at 5 (the mint family and name-only tokens), 13 at 4, 23 at 3 and 16 at 2, with dated patterns for x420's repo owner, APINow.fun's founder, Capminal, vAPI Network, Otonix, AInalyst, $TRUST ME BROs and Halo among others.
11. Where people are named the evidence is their own public repos and sites; no pseudonymous person or deployer wallet was linked to a real-world identity, and funding was mostly not found.

## 2. PING ($PING): the first x402 mint on Base

**Verdict:** Speculative (an experiment, not a project). **Momentum:** Dead as a project; the token merely persists.
**Trend-hopping score:** 5 on the rubric's own terms (narrative-only, no product). There is no persistent team, so no documented hopping: a one-off riding the x402 launch narrative.
**What it is:** An anonymous memecoin whose supply was "minted" by paying 1 USDC per request through x402 (via the x402scan interface), launched in October 2025 as a protocol "stress test". Its site says "No utility. No team. No financial advice." [ping.observer](https://ping.observer/)

**Token facts**
- Ticker PING; Base only; contract 0xd85c31854c2B0Fb40aaA9E2Fc4Da23C21f829d46. [BaseScan](https://basescan.org/address/0xd85c31854c2B0Fb40aaA9E2Fc4Da23C21f829d46)
- Deployed 2025-10-18 12:36:53 UTC (block 37,000,833) by EOA 0xdbd148a34491c39b7b1a42a539f62f82bbbba43e in a direct contract creation (not Clanker, not Zora, no factory). [creation tx](https://basescan.org/tx/0x221f65c0863e75aaddb112e2c60ed868d5f9530dbdc7a579b7445b3b614b31e1) First x402 payments 2025-10-19 (633 from 591 payers); first Uniswap v4 pool (USDC pair) 2025-10-22 15:35 UTC. Press dates the launch 2025-10-22 or 2025-10-23.
- Method: "x402 mint" fair launch. `totalSupply()` is 1,000,000,000 PING, which equals 100,000 mints x 5,000 PING plus a 500,000,000 PING pool seed. `mintCount()` = `maxMintCount()` = 100,000, so no further mint is possible.
- Controls: `owner()` is the zero address (renounced 2025-10-22 20:57:07 UTC), but AccessControl roles remain. `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE`: the deployer and 0xEc042EA3...33cb (granted 2025-11-10 21:53 UTC). `MINTER_ROLE`: the deployer and 0xb828c5Cd...75FD. The admin role can call `collectLpFees` and `withdrawToken` (any ERC-20 held by the contract); no function withdraws the pool principal. [Sourcify](https://sourcify.dev/server/v2/contract/8453/0xd85c31854c2B0Fb40aaA9E2Fc4Da23C21f829d46?fields=sources,abi)

**How a payment became a mint.** Payments went to the token contract, but a `MINTER_ROLE` account then called `batchMint`, which records each payment hash and mints 5,000 PING per hash. The deployer granted that role to 0xb828c5Cd...75FD on 2025-10-18 13:00:17 UTC (23 minutes after deployment) and sent it ETH. Sampled batches on 2025-10-22 held 70, 55, 78 and 72 mints, about one every 14 to 16 seconds. The site's "100% of the USDC collected was automatically used to provide liquidity" omits this off-chain minter and the later withdrawal. Odaily (2025-10-24) was right that an off-chain service decided which payments counted, but its claim that the USDC went to a treasury wallet set by x402scan is not what the chain shows. The researcher found nothing linking x402scan to the minter and makes no claim about who ran it. [Odaily](https://www.odaily.news/en/post/5207078), [Blockscout](https://base.blockscout.com/address/0xb828c5Cde35f51E4072623e4F5fD9817Fd9d75FD)

**Traction.** x402scan counts 138,465 payments, $138,454, from 19,908 payers (about 7 each, which points to scripted repeat minting), 2025-10-19 to 2025-10-27. 124,095 landed on 2025-10-22, 97.2% of that day's 127,661 Base x402 transactions. 38,465 payments (about $38.5k) came after the 100,000 cap and no refund was found. The contract paid out 177,574.11 USDC (100,000 to the pool at 15:35:17 UTC on 2025-10-22, 77,574.11 withdrawn) and holds 0, so about $39k more reached it than x402scan counts; the gap is unexplained. Market cap passed $57M on 2025-10-25 and fell below $30M on 2025-10-27. [Jinse via Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/news/detail/12560605057035) Now: 127,553 holders (2026-10-10), FDV about $0.46M, pool liquidity about $257k, 24h volume under $5k. [GeckoTerminal](https://api.geckoterminal.com/api/v2/networks/base/tokens/0xd85c31854c2B0Fb40aaA9E2Fc4Da23C21f829d46/info) Chainalysis (2026-06-03): PING-driven wallets showed 87% weekly retention for one week, then 5%. [Chainalysis](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/x402-agentic-payments-adoption/)

**Team.** Anonymous; no funding disclosed. FAQ: "No. $PING has no team, no roadmap, no utility, and no allowlist."
- X @pingobserver: linked from the official site and from OpenSea and GeckoTerminal token info; high confidence it is the project account; no person behind it is named. [OpenSea](https://opensea.io/token/base/0xd85c31854c2b0fb40aaa9e2fc4da23c21f829d46)
- Farcaster @pingobserver (fid 1402447, 8 followers, bio repeats "First coin launched on x402"): not linked by the site, so moderate confidence (matching handle and text). [Warpcast API](https://api.warpcast.com/v2/user-by-username?username=pingobserver)
- GitHub: none linked; at least four look-alike repos copy the site (for example piraunxx/ping.observer, last pushed 2025-10-25) and are treated as unrelated. Telegram: none. Some explainers call it an "AI-agent monitoring" project; the site says nothing of the kind, and the researcher reads that as a misreading of the account name (inference).

**What happened next.** The deployer called `collectLpFees` eight times by 2025-10-24 08:25. On 2025-11-17 10:32:37 it granted admin to 0x88CaB199...FD12, which at 10:35:21 and 10:36:57 called `withdrawToken` for 1 USDC, then 77,573.114567 USDC, to itself, and renounced the role at 10:50:03. That account made further USDC transfers on 2025-11-17, 2025-11-28 and 2026-01-08/12 (destinations not traced). [Blockscout](https://base.blockscout.com/api/v2/addresses/0xd85c31854c2B0Fb40aaA9E2Fc4Da23C21f829d46/token-transfers?type=ERC-20&filter=from) In the last six months nothing shipped; `api.ping.observer` returns NXDOMAIN (removal date unknown) while the apex site serves HTTP 200. [Google DNS](https://dns.google/resolve?name=api.ping.observer&type=A) Cointelegraph argues the lasting effect was the influx of developers experimenting with x402, not the price; Chainalysis says the Q4 2025 weekly surge (over 10,000%) was "driven largely by meme coin activity, especially PING".

**Opinion (researcher).** PING deserves a place in the x402 story as the first public "mint by paying through x402" event, but it is not a builder and has no direction. The checkable parts of its design held: fixed supply, protocol-owned pool, renounced Ownable owner. The parts the site left out matter more. Minting ran through a privileged off-chain account, the admin keys were never given up, and about 44% of the USDC that passed through the contract ended up with an admin-role account instead of in the pool. It proved that anyone can point x402 at a token contract and run a mint service, and that most of the resulting "transactions" were repeat mints, not API payments. Not confirmed: whether the 77,574 USDC covered refunds, costs or profit.

## 3. Mint and launch tools

Three kinds of tool exist on Base and none has become durable: the PING mint template, launch-as-a-service sites that take an x402 payment and deploy through Clanker or their own ERC-20s, and copies on other chains. Clanker is the usual plumbing, with x402 as the payment gate in front; Heurist's machine is the exception. The researcher found no x402 mint integration at Clanker or Zora themselves (Zora's only x402 feature found is the buyer-side `zora pay` command, v1.6.0, 2026-06-25, per a sibling researcher's search). Copying after the PING mint: x420 token 2025-10-23; Heurist repo 2025-10-24; x402.team tokens 2025-10-25; Heurist contracts 2025-10-27; SpeedRun post 2025-10-30; REGENT 2025-11-06.

## 7. Conflicts and gaps

**Corrections the notes make to the brief's leads**
- PING is confirmed as the first x402 mint on Base. It started 2025-10-19 and the bulk landed on 2025-10-21 and 2025-10-22, not 2025-10-23 as several articles say. "x402-branded tokens existed before PING" is also confirmed (see 4C); none was a mint.
- PING's site says the contract minted automatically and 100% of the USDC went to locked liquidity. The chain shows an operator-run minter, $100,000 to the pool and $77,574 withdrawn by an admin-role account.
- The mint tokens do not have "differing bytecodes": BIP, MOOM, x420DOG and MAI share PING's verified source, and 叉肆零贰 is a factory variant.
- HTTP402's launcher is 0xa52ad458ce0282a971ecc71c051a32f28946bb9f (B20LaunchpadFactory), not 0x826a2b79...; only HTTPBASE20 used 0x826a2b79... (a fork named Clanker).

- Quick Intel "Tator": the termo.ai listing returns 404 and its "10% interface fee" and "Flaunch" claims are not in Quick Intel's sources. Heurist made 20 launches (5 graduated), not "unknown". Token Metrics AI [OLD] carries the x402 tag on a legacy listing.
