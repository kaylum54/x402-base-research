# Digest: x402 apps, trust services and data/AI sellers on Base

Prepared 2026-10-11 for the report writer, in place of the raw notes. It condenses two due diligence notes, both dated 2026-10-11, with no new research. **Apps note** = `dd_x402_apps_and_trust.md` (Zauth, Cred Protocol, Snack Money, Fluora and other x402-native apps). **Data note** = `dd_data_ai_sellers.md` (Gloria AI, Otto AI, AIXBT, Cookie DAO, Venice and other data or AI sellers). Window for "last six months": 2026-04-11 to 2026-10-11. The notes accessed their sources on 2026-10-11 unless a block says otherwise. Token facts only, no price analysis.

**How to read it**
- Seriousness and momentum labels are the researchers' own (Serious builder, Credible but small, Speculative, Likely abandoned).
- Trend-hopping score: 1 = long-running focus and x402 is a natural extension; 3 = several narrative pivots but a real product each time; 5 = narrative-only branding.
- Usage numbers are USDC on Base from x402scan unless stated. tx = transactions. 30d = last 30 days. payTo = the receiving wallet that x402scan tracks. Buyers and payers = unique sender addresses.
- **Conflict:** marks a point where the two notes disagree, or a project's claim disagrees with the check. **Unverified:** marks a claim the researcher could not confirm.
- Blocks run from serious builders to speculative projects. Dead, dormant, no-x402 and thinly covered projects are in the closing table, with the details worth keeping.

## Network context and data caveats

- **Where usage numbers come from.** x402scan's public tRPC endpoints, queried 2026-10-11 (`public.sellers.bazaar.list`, `public.stats.bucketed`, `public.stats.overall`, `public.transfers.list`, `public.origins.search`) ([router source](https://github.com/Merit-Systems/x402scan/tree/main/apps/scan/src/trpc/routers/public)). Only origins in x402scan's Bazaar list can be matched to a named project. x402scan's paid REST API answers HTTP 402 and nobody paid. Manual tracking page patterns: `/server/{originId}`, `/recipient/{payTo}`, `/facilitator/{id}`, `/buyer/{address}` ([route files](https://github.com/Merit-Systems/x402scan/tree/main/apps/scan/src/app/%28app%29)).
- **Base by month (tx / USDC):** Jan 12.1M / $1.20M; Feb 1.4M / $2.06M; Mar 1.8M / $2.27M; Apr 2.3M / $1.93M; May 3.0M / $1.03M; Jun 12.8M / $0.79M; Jul 11.9M / $0.65M; Aug 9.1M / $1.03M; Sep 2.2M / $0.57M; 1-11 Oct 0.72M / $0.25M ([x402scan](https://www.x402scan.com/api/trpc/public.stats.bucketed)). Last 30 days: Base 2.17M tx and $702k; Solana 491k and $362k; all chains 2.66M and $1.06M. Data note, Base 30d: $702,249, 35,307 buyers, 39,328 sellers; 1,214 Base origins active in 30d and 2,433 all-time; the top 99 registered origins sum to $145,768 and the top 3 are 75% of that.
- **Conflict:** x402.org's homepage (page dated 2026-08-25) shows 75.41M tx, $24.24M, 94.06K buyers and 22K sellers for 30 days ([x402.org](https://x402.org)). The Apps note could not reconcile it with x402scan (different date or scope). Transaction counts are inflated by micro-payment bursts, and dollars are small.
- **Wash screen.** Apps note: share of the latest 100 and largest 100 transfers from one wallet; up to 40 sampled payers checked on Base RPC (nonce 0 and no ETH is normal for gasless EIP-3009 payers, so only a weak signal); regularity (identical daily counts, fixed prices); whether prices make product sense. Data note: distinct and repeat payers, top-payer share, identical amounts from a fixed wallet set, single-wallet ping traffic, abrupt starts and stops. These show patterns, not intent.
- **Main finding.** Organic x402 revenue on Base is in the low thousands of dollars a month per app, so ranking by transaction count rewards bots. Beyond Venice and Cluster, no data or AI seller with a Base token shows more than about $100 of 30d revenue (AIXBT $0, Pocket $71, AUBRAI $63, Otto $30, Gloria $1). The largest sellers with real usage, such as BlockRun, have no token.
- **Unmapped large Base recipients** (no x402scan origin, not identified): `0x93862e5b2b1fa10a01772e7e9ca7cdc7deb5ca25` (1,632 tx, $22.6k, 151 buyers in 30d, last payment 2026-09-28, mostly $18 payments) and `0xffde1acbac169f822dabc3d97d6d7127dcae6003` (23 tx, $35.7k, 14 buyers, tickets of $585-$2,299). Seven Meridian-facilitated recipients each took about $19k from only 5 buyers in 30d, which also looks like test or wash flow (Meridian is covered by another researcher).
- **Limits.** Both researchers ran out of WebSearch budget. x.com is not readable, so X bios are unverified; handles come from official sites, GitHub profile fields, Virtuals verified links, CoinGecko and docs. Crunchbase, CryptoRank, pump.fun and Blockscout blocked requests and CoinGecko's API was rate-limited (Apps note); funding databases could not be searched (Data note). People research covers public professional information only. Anonymous people were not identified, and pseudonymous handles are not linked to real identities unless a source says so.

BlockRun and Venice lead: BlockRun ships almost daily (113 ClawRouter releases since 2026-04-01) and has the one large open-source audience, but its paid volume fell about 95% from September to early October. Venice has a $65M Series A, a token tied to revenue and real repeat payers, yet x402 is only $200-600 a month of ordinary top-ups. Laso Finance is the clearest case of an x402 payment buying a real-world product, at about $1-3k a month on Base. Stalling or dead: Cred Protocol (x402 total $0.88 on Base), Gloria AI, Fluora and Snack Money; Zauth's x402 side has shipped nothing since February 2026. Trend-hopping scores of 4 or more: Zauth, Otto AI, Cluster Protocol, BotPay, AX1 and claw402, with X Pay at 5. Cluster (campaign-like), KORUS (unproven), BotPay (scripted), AX1 (engagement farming), X Pay (likely self-funded) and claw402 (bot-dominated) post volume that is not a demand signal, so do not rank by transaction count. Watch BlockRun's releases and Base/Solana split daily, Venice weekly, Laso and Cred monthly, and KORUS in two to four weeks for repeat buyers and a named team.
