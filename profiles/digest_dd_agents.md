# Digest: agent frameworks, apps and platforms building for x402 on Base

Prepared 2026-10-11 from two due-diligence notes of the same date: `dd_agent_frameworks.md` (Daydreams, Virtuals Protocol, elizaOS) and `dd_agent_apps_platforms.md` (Questflow, Bankr, Olas, Zora, AWE Network, BlockRun, FluxA, plus screened and discovered projects). No new research was done. "Last six months" means 2026-04-11 to 2026-10-11. No price or chart analysis. Trend-hopping is scored 1 to 5; a higher score means more narrative-chasing.

**Limits that apply to every block**
- Web search ran out of budget in both notes. A few items come from search snippets only and are flagged where they appear.
- X was not fetchable, so X-post cadence and X-only announcements are missing. Crunchbase, RootData, CryptoRank and Dealroom were not reachable (403 or empty), so "no funding found" means not found, not none.
- Pseudonymous people were not linked to real-world identities. An X handle appears only where the person or an official page declared it, and the verification note says how.
- x402scan counts are the transfers it indexes (USDC). Tickets are tiny (BlockRun about $0.017 per transaction all-time, Questflow's facilitator about $0.02), so transaction counts overstate value.

**Conflicts between the two notes**
- Virtuals x402 volume: the frameworks notes give 1,993,122 transactions and US$4.34M all-time (x402scan `virtuals`); the Olas notes quote "about 2.4M x402scan tx, $4.9M volume". Not reconciled.
- Pace of the x402 reference repo: 67 to 115 commits a month in the frameworks notes, "roughly 65-115" in the apps notes.
- The apps notes list Daydreams Router as a BlockRun competitor; the frameworks notes find the Router offline (NXDOMAIN).

**Links between projects (from the notes)**
- AWE's AWEtoAgent-Kit lists Daydreams `lucid-agents` contributors (beauwilliams, ponderingdemocritus, RedBeardEth). The AWE notes infer it is derived from Daydreams code; the Daydreams notes do not mention it.

## Group view

Olas, Bankr and BlockRun lead among the builders. Olas has the deepest engineering and restarted x402 work in September-October 2026, but it uses x402 as a buyer and has no open seller side yet. Bankr sells x402 as a priced product, which Questflow and Olas do not, but it publishes no usage numbers. BlockRun is the clearest company built around x402 (27,574,606 transactions on its Base origin at about $0.017 each), and FluxA is second to Coinbase by 30-day facilitator volume (US$322,401). Virtuals and elizaOS are large and active, but x402 is a side rail for both: Virtuals' x402 volume fell about 99% after April 2026, and elizaOS shows no x402 usage and has a token its founder declared dead on 2026-08-05. Daydreams, Zora and AWE are slowing or stalled on x402, and Questflow has most likely left it. Questflow, AWE and elizaOS score 4 for trend-hopping; Daydreams, Virtuals and Zora score 3. For a daily watch, the apps notes rank Olas first, Bankr second, then BlockRun and FluxA, with Zora as a one-off mention and Questflow dropped; add the two Daydreams ERC pull requests and the Task Market completed-task count, Virtuals' monthly x402 volume, and the elizaOS x402 commit stream.
