# Cloudflare's x402 and agent-payment products, and what large companies have shipped with x402 (as of 2026-10-11)

Scope and method. Coinbase, Base and the x402 spec are covered by another researcher; rival protocols (ACP, Visa TAP, Mastercard Agent Pay) appear only where they connect to x402. Almost every claim below comes from a page I opened directly (Cloudflare blog, docs, changelog and earnings transcripts; Linux Foundation; AWS; Stripe docs; Circle; Visa; Mastercard). Part-way through, the web-search tool hit its per-turn budget (shared across researchers), so later checks were direct page fetches only; what that left unchecked is listed under Gaps. x.com cannot be opened by my tools, so X handles were verified through official sites (Cloudflare blog author cards) or self-declared GitHub profile fields. To respect quoting limits I paraphrase official wording and use exactly one direct quote (section 1); the linked pages hold the exact text. Cloudflare blog JSON-LD shows a dateModified of 2026-07-15 on many 2025 posts; that looks like a site-wide re-index, so I use datePublished only. Status labels follow the brief: shipped, beta, announced, rumoured.

## 1. The claim: a closed-beta Cloudflare product, "Gateway Monetization", that lets agents pay autonomously

### Takeaway
The product is Monetization Gateway (the two words are reversed in the claim): a US-only closed beta since 2026-09-30 (waitlist from 2026-07-01) that lets sites, APIs and MCP tools charge agents per request over x402, settling in USDC on Base through Coinbase's facilitator. It is the seller side; the agent wallet that would make agent payments autonomous is a separate product, Cloudflare Wallets, which only offers handle reservation so far, and very early is fair for the Gateway even though x402 and Cloudflare's SDK support for it are over a year old.

### Cited Findings

**Name, dates, status**
- The developer changelog entry dated 2026-09-30 states: "Monetization Gateway is now available in closed beta." It names the product's use as charging agents for APIs, MCP tools, sites and datasets, and says access is requested from the dashboard. [Cloudflare changelog](https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/post/2026-09-30-closed-beta/)
- First announcement 2026-07-01 (authors Rohin Lohe, Justin Ridgely, Will Papper). At that point it was a waitlist with several features described as planned, not a launched product. [Announcing the Monetization Gateway](https://blog.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway/); authors and dates per [Cloudflare x402 tag page](https://blog.cloudflare.com/tag/x402/)
- Closed-beta post 2026-09-30 (Rohin Lohe, Jorge Silva, Caleb Carithers, Isaac Bremseth); it says the idea was announced three months earlier. [Monetization Gateway beta](https://blog.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway-beta/); [x402 tag page](https://blog.cloudflare.com/tag/x402/)
- It was one of the Birthday Week 2026 items (listed for Wed 2026-09-30; wrap-up published 2026-10-05). [Birthday Week 2026 wrap-up](https://blog.cloudflare.com/birthday-week-2026-wrap-up/)
- Docs: [developers.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway](https://developers.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway/) with sub-pages [Eligibility](https://developers.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway/eligibility/), [Get started](https://developers.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway/get-started/), [x402](https://developers.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway/x402/) and [Configuration](https://developers.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway/configuration/). The docs label it closed beta. Cloudflare's x402 contact mailbox is x402@cloudflare.com (linked in the beta post). [docs hub](https://developers.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway/); [beta post](https://blog.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway-beta/)

**How it works**
- The seller writes rules (match on URL, headers, query parameters or caller attributes, for example only bots verified by BotBase), sets a price and a receiving wallet. A buyer requests the resource, gets a 402 with payment requirements, signs a payment authorization and retries. The gateway verifies it, passes a signed payment context to the origin, settles through the Coinbase x402 Facilitator, then returns the resource. [Monetization Gateway docs](https://developers.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway/); [Get started](https://developers.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway/get-started/)
- Protocol details: x402 version 2, scheme `exact` for fixed prices and `upto` for variable prices (authorize a maximum, origin reports the actual amount). The origin must verify a signed JWT in the `PAYMENT-CONTEXT` header; the gateway settles the maximum if the reported amount is higher, and nothing if it is zero. [x402 page](https://developers.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway/x402/); [payment validation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway/configuration/payment-validation/)
- Settlement is USDC on Base (beta post). The July post only said stablecoins, named Open USD and USDC as examples and did not name a chain. [beta post](https://blog.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway-beta/); [July 1 post](https://blog.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway/)
- Payments go peer-to-peer to the seller's wallet; sellers can keep stablecoins or redeem them to a bank account; the buyer needs no account with the seller. [July 1 post](https://blog.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway/)
- It is not NET Dollar and not pay per crawl: the beta post mentions neither. Pay Per Use (a separate beta for publishers) was launched the same day. [beta post](https://blog.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway-beta/); [Pay Per Use post](https://blog.cloudflare.com/pay-per-use/)

**Pricing and fees**
- I found no Cloudflare fee for the Gateway on any page read (blog posts, docs hub, get-started, x402 and payment-validation pages). The docs give one number: a minimum price of $0.001 per request. [Get started](https://developers.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway/get-started/)
- Search Engine Journal reported a $0.001 to $100 settlement range; I could not find the $100 ceiling in the docs. [SEJ, 2026-10-01](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/cloudflare-moves-monetization-gateway-pay-per-use-into-beta/591719/)
- The facilitator is Coinbase's. Its published price: free up to 1,000 onchain transactions a month, then $0.001 each; verification is free. Which party bears that fee in the Gateway is not stated. [CDP facilitator docs](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/x402/seller/facilitator)
- Seller-set pricing modes named in the beta post: fixed (Ceramic.ai), variable (API2PDF) and origin-controlled (Cloudflare AI Gateway). [beta post](https://blog.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway-beta/)

**Eligibility (restrictive)**
- Buyers and sellers must both be US-based. Sellers must accept the service terms, keep a credit card on file, verify their email, have an account older than 60 days and pass account security checks; the zone must be proxied through Cloudflare, be older than 30 days and pass zone checks. No separate buyer requirements are listed. [Eligibility page](https://developers.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway/eligibility/)
- Cloudflare says other geographies are coming, without dates. [beta post](https://blog.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway-beta/)

**Who is using it in the beta**
- Four named users: Cloudflare AI Gateway (pay-per-request inference), Ceramic.ai (web search per query, no API key), Stocktwits (market sentiment data) and API2PDF (document conversion API; Cloudflare says conversion fell by more than half under the old card sign-up flow, a figure from Cloudflare's own post). [beta post](https://blog.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway-beta/)
- Roadmap stated by Cloudflare: make services discoverable to agents, expose logs for all transactions, add payment rails, add identity primitives. [beta post](https://blog.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway-beta/)
- No transaction counts or payouts were published for either the Gateway or Pay Per Use. [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/cloudflare-moves-monetization-gateway-pay-per-use-into-beta/591719/)

**Things the claim may be blending with it**
- Cloudflare AI Gateway Machine Payments, a separate beta (changelog 2026-09-30): clients pay for inference on selected open models from a stablecoin wallet using x402, sent with the `Payment-Method: x402` header on `/ai/run`. Requires a US base and a card on file. The docs list four open models. [AI Gateway changelog](https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/post/2026-09-30-machine-payments/); [AI Gateway docs](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-gateway/features/machine-payments/)
- Cloudflare Wallets, the agent-side product (section 2): announced 2026-08-04; the docs (last updated 2026-08-19) say only handle reservation is live and a handle cannot yet send, receive or hold funds. [Wallets blog](https://blog.cloudflare.com/wallets/); [Wallets docs](https://developers.cloudflare.com/wallets/)

**The claim against the record**

| Claim | Check |
|---|---|

| Released in closed beta | Correct, 2026-09-30. Waitlist since 2026-07-01. |
| Lets agents make autonomous payments | Partly. It lets sellers charge agents; each agent signs a per-request payment authorization. The first-party agent wallet (Cloudflare Wallets) is a separate product and not funded or spendable yet. Buyers need an x402-capable client or wallet; Cloudflare's Agents SDK has had an x402 client since 2025-09-23. |
| Very early | Fair for the Gateway: closed beta, US-only, four launch users, no published volumes. Not fair for x402 itself (Visa's data article dates its launch to May 2025, per [Visa](https://www.visa.com/en-us/thought-leadership/innovation/agentic-payments-from-the-ground-up)) or Cloudflare's Agents SDK support (2025-09-23, per the [Cloudflare x402 post](https://blog.cloudflare.com/x402/)). |
| Missing from the claim | US-only restriction; seller requirements; x402 v2 over USDC on Base via Coinbase's facilitator; no published Cloudflare fee; $0.001 minimum; separate sister products (Wallets, AI Gateway Machine Payments, Pay Per Use, pay per crawl). |

### Inferences
- "Gateway Monetization" may be a blend of AI Gateway (one of the Gateway's launch users, and a separate machine-payments beta) and Monetization Gateway; both shipped on the same day.
- The accurate one-line version for a video: Cloudflare opened a closed beta (US only) that lets website and API owners charge AI agents per request using x402 and USDC on Base; the matching agent wallet is announced but not live.
- Cloudflare's CFO described Wallets in the present tense on 2026-09-09 (section 2), ahead of what the docs showed, so executive phrasing runs ahead of shipped product.

### Gaps
- Whether Cloudflare takes a fee or who pays the facilitator fee is unknown; I did not find service-specific terms for the Gateway (the Application Services terms page has no section for it).
- The $100 per-request ceiling reported by SEJ is unconfirmed in docs.
- Number of sellers or buyers admitted, and any transaction volume, is unknown.
- I do not know when the claim was made, so I cannot say whether it describes the July waitlist or the September beta.

## 2. Cloudflare's related products, status, and what executives have said (2025 to October 2026)

### Takeaway
Cloudflare is building identity, policy, seller-payment and buyer-payment layers around HTTP 402 and is protocol-agnostic (x402, MPP, a fiat batch-settlement binding). Live: AI Crawl Control, Agents SDK and MCP x402 support, signed agents; beta: pay per crawl (since 2025-07-01), Monetization Gateway, AI Gateway Machine Payments, Pay Per Use; partial: Wallets; not launched: NET Dollar; and executive statements run ahead of the product.

### Cited Findings

**Pay per crawl (status: closed/private beta, unchanged for 15 months)**
- Introduced 2025-07-01 as a private beta: crawlers hitting a paid URL get a 402 with a price header, can send max-price or exact-price headers, sign requests with Web Bot Auth, and Cloudflare acts as merchant of record, aggregates charges and pays publishers. [Introducing pay per crawl](https://blog.cloudflare.com/introducing-pay-per-crawl/); launched alongside default blocking of AI crawlers for new domains, per the [press release, 2025-07-01](https://www.cloudflare.com/press/press-releases/2025/cloudflare-just-changed-how-ai-crawlers-scrape-the-internet-at-large/)
- The docs (updated 2026-07-28) still call it a closed beta, with sign-up via a form or the account executive for Enterprise; they do not mention x402 or the Monetization Gateway. [Pay per crawl docs](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-crawl-control/features/pay-per-crawl/what-is-pay-per-crawl/)
- Dated changelog items: 2025-07-01 launch (payments via Stripe), 2025-12-10 Discovery API and custom pricing (private beta), 2026-06-16 URI exemptions and dynamic pricing. [AI Crawl Control changelog feed](https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/rss/ai-crawl-control.xml); [2026-06-16 entry](https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/post/2026-06-16-pay-per-crawl-advanced-configuration/)
- Link to x402: in the 2025-09-23 post Cloudflare proposed a `deferred` x402 scheme (signed commitment, settled later) to bring to pay per crawl. In the x402 repo there is now a batch-settlement scheme with a Cloudflare network binding (id cloudflare:402, version 1.0.0, initial release dated 2026-01): signed commitments (HTTP Message Signatures), off-chain batched settlement, USD, no gas, Cloudflare as trusted merchant of record. The doc carries no status label and names no authors. [Cloudflare x402 post](https://blog.cloudflare.com/x402/); [cloudflare binding spec](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/coinbase/x402/main/specs/schemes/batch-settlement/batch_settlement_cloudflare.md); [generic batch-settlement spec](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/coinbase/x402/main/specs/schemes/batch-settlement/batch_settlement.md)

**AI Crawl Control (status: shipped, all plans)**
- Became generally available on 2025-08-27 (blog 2025-08-28), renamed from AI Audit, with custom 402 responses when blocking crawlers. [changelog feed](https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/rss/ai-crawl-control.xml); [Will Allen's post list](https://blog.cloudflare.com/author/will-allen/)
- The product page says it works on all plans and lists pay per crawl as a private-beta option (page updated 2026-08-14). [AI Crawl Control docs](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-crawl-control/)
- On 2026-02-04 the docs added an x402 payment-gated proxy template for charging bots. [changelog feed](https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/rss/ai-crawl-control.xml)

**Agents SDK, MCP and docs (status: shipped; MPP added by 2026-08-05)**

[Agentic payments docs](https://developers.cloudflare.com/agents/tools/payments/); [MPP docs](https://developers.cloudflare.com/agents/tools/payments/mpp/); [mpp.dev](https://mpp.dev/)

### Inferences
- Cloudflare is positioning as the toll booth plus identity layer for agent traffic and is hedging on rails: x402 over USDC on Base for the Gateway, MPP compatibility in its SDK, and a fiat merchant-of-record binding (cloudflare:402) for pay per crawl.
- NET Dollar looks deprioritized: no 2026 Cloudflare product page uses it, executives stopped naming it after October 2025, and Cloudflare joined Open USD and uses USDC. This is an inference; Cloudflare has not said it dropped NET Dollar.
- The 402 count Cloudflare cites (over a billion a day in 2025-09, over two billion in 2026-06) is mostly block responses, not payments; Cohen said as much. A custom 402 block response has been available since 2025-08-27.
- Worth watching: Gateway leaving closed beta or leaving the US; Wallets funding and spending going live; first published volumes; pay per crawl leaving closed beta; AI Gateway Machine Payments adding models; NET Dollar or Open USD showing up in a Cloudflare product page; the Q3 2026 earnings call (date not confirmed); IETF webbotauth outputs.

### Inferences
- Only a handful of companies have product work behind their x402 support (AWS, Circle, Stripe in preview, Vercel, Cloudflare in beta); everyone else's visible commitment is Foundation membership and quotes.
- Stripe and Visa are hedging toward MPP, which they co-own or support; the card networks are bridging identity (via Web Bot Auth) rather than using x402 itself.
- OpenAI and Anthropic being absent from the Foundation, and OpenAI's maintainers declining a built-in tool, suggests no near-term native x402 in their products (inference from sources above).
- AWS GA is the clearest signal that x402 is reaching managed enterprise tooling; it relies on Coinbase and Stripe/Privy as wallet providers.
